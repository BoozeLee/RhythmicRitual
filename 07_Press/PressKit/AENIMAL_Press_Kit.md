# Rhythmic Ritual 01: AENIMAL Press Kit

## Short Description

Rhythmic Ritual 01: AENIMAL is a mechanical-jungle club concept in Vlijtingen, built as the spiritual successor to Animinimals. The first edition blends psytrance, techno, disco, and groovy rhythms with a cyberpunk jungle visual world and a clear message: welcome home.

## Boilerplate

Rhythmic Ritual is a six-edition underground club movement connecting local Belgian scene memory with a future-facing Vlijtingen-to-Berlin ambition. Edition 01, AENIMAL, rebuilds the Animinimals spirit as a living machine: organic memory, exposed circuitry, ritual rhythm, and an inclusive door for veterans and first-timers alike.
