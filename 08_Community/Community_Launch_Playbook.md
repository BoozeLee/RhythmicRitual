# AENIMAL Community Launch Playbook

## Purpose

Turn the campaign audience into a room before the event happens. The community should feel like a signal channel, not a generic promo feed.

## Tone

- Direct.
- Welcoming.
- Underground but not exclusive.
- Scene-aware without gatekeeping.
- Future-facing without losing the Animinimals memory.

## Weekly Community Actions

- Week 1: Ask for old Zoo memories.
- Week 2: Ask what "welcome home" means in nightlife.
- Week 3: Share v2 rooftop ritual panels and ask which frame feels strongest.
- Week 4: Share v3 character pass and ask for costume/decor reactions.
- Week 5: Ask for track references.
- Week 6: Collect short testimonials.
- Week 7: Announce local activation / QR clue concept.
- Week 8: Share final logistics and access reminder.

## Moderation Rules

- No harassment.
- No racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, or age-based gatekeeping.
- No spam or unrelated promotion.
- Respect the venue and local community.
- Keep access/ticket resale out of the main channel.
